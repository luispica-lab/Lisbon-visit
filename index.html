LX Factory

R. Rodrigues de Faria 103, Alcântara · not a Wave 1 site
ContextOn foot
Why we finish here
A nineteenth-century industrial complex turned creative quarter — studios, independent retail, restaurants and bars, and one of the highest-rated destinations in the city. It is not in the development plan, but it is the clearest single place to see the customer the plan is built around. The Chiado business case rests on creative-economy workers from exactly this cluster, and standing in it explains that better than a slide can.
What to look at
  • The tenant mix and price points, which are the closest thing Lisbon has to the J&TJ customer at rest.
  • How independent operators here handle counter service and outdoor seating.
  • Whether the site itself merits a later-wave conversation — high footfall, strong demographic, and no incumbent in our category. It is not in Wave 1 and we are not proposing it today.

The competitive picture

In Portugal J&TJ would not face one direct competitor. It would face four fronts: scale and routine from Starbucks, healthy urban lifestyle from Honest Greens, and coffee credibility from a strong specialty layer concentrated in exactly the neighbourhoods on this tour.

How a site gets approved
Hard criteria

J&TJ Retail Tag fit. 80–120 m² standard, 90–150 m² flagship. Frontage 6 m+ flagship, 4.5 m+ standard. Ceiling 3.5 m+ flagship. Footfall 25k+ pedestrians per day on street. Corner or main-intersection visibility. Rent below 12% of conservative Y2 sales, target below 10%. Minimum 800 m from any existing J&TJ.

Soft criteria

Brand-defining adjacencies — premium retail, gyms, hotels, theatres. Terrace possible. Tech-friendly, with delivery zone, scooter and pickup access. Walkable to gyms, co-working and universities. Photogenic façade. Repeat-use base of residents, commuters or students.

Governance

Internal scouting plus an outsourced opening partner and two external brokers per cluster. Pre-screening by the Brand Manager. Hard-criteria checklist signed off and presented to the Board. Full A&FC business case in Retail Platform format.

No exceptions

No site goes live without J&TJ A&FC approval. Post-opening review at month six against the business case.

Practical notes
Tuk-tuks

Three vehicles for twelve guests. Open-sided and unbeatable through the narrow streets of Bairro Alto and Príncipe Real, where a coach cannot go. Slow on the big avenues, which is why the route runs one way across the city rather than back and forth. Conversation is difficult while moving, so the tuk-tuks pull over at each site and the commentary happens stationary.

August

Expect 28–33 °C in the afternoon. The tuk-tuks are shaded but open. Water is carried throughout, and the Ajuda climb before lunch is the most exposed leg of the day.

On foot

Only the Chiado to Baixa leg is walked. Lisbon's limestone pavements are slippery, and comfortable shoes matter more here than in most cities.

Finish

Hotel drop-off around 17:25 rather than 17:00, to fit Belém and LX Factory into the afternoon. That still leaves two hours before dinner.
